# Pricing

A Fast Chat uses a one-time purchase plus prepaid usage credits.

## Plan

- **$17 one-time fee** for lifetime access.
- Includes **$5 of usage credit**.
- Users can buy more usage credit when needed.

## How it works

Users spend usage credit when they use hosted AI models.

When their balance reaches **$0**, model usage is blocked until they buy more credit.

## Billing flow

- Polar handles purchases and payments.
- A Fast Chat tracks access, usage, and remaining balance.
- The initial purchase unlocks the app and grants included credit.
- Top-ups add more usage credit.

## Product principle

Users pay once for the app, then pay only for the model usage they consume.
